Modifications in this fixture:
- The current project glyph masks and typesetter were used with explicitly
  curated white-bubble fills. This does not claim automatic cleanup performance
  on this page.
- The adjacent exclamation was separated from one Japanese source span, and
  the small final handwritten note was added as a presentation text region.
- Sound effects and artwork outside the selected dialogue masks remain intact.
- Shopkeeper and Meru are curated demonstration identities, manually associated
  with eleven clear dialogue regions in character-seeds.json. These labels are
  not Magi detections, embeddings or model predictions. The initial ellipsis
  and abrupt exclamation remain unassigned because their attribution is less
  certain. No voice or speaker style is preselected.

This fixture is excluded from research inputs, training and evaluation.
The editable application copy is created under data/presentation-demo and uses
its own memory.sqlite3; reopening it preserves user corrections.
Missing curated characters/bindings are added only for this exact original and
demo scope. Existing names, voices and all bindings (including an explicit
Unknown choice, whether confirmed or not) always take precedence. Source text
or geometry changed by the user is not given a new default binding.
All three pages share this isolated character database. Adding continuation
pages never replaces the original single-page session or any saved page edits.
